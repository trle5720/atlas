Mission

Foster social justice, health and gender equity through culturally relevant physical, mental and social wellness programs.

Vision

We envision a world where gender, race, ethnicity, disability, education, income, origin and sexual orientation are no longer predictors of life and health outcomes.

Our Story

Founded in 2017, ViVe advances equity by addressing systemic barriers faced by low-income, racially, and ethnically diverse communities in Denver and surrounding cities. Primarily serving underserved Latino migrants and other marginalized communities, ViVe provides programs that foster health equity through culturally relevant wellness activities.

Through partnerships with local organizations and donations secured through grants and private funding, ViVe Wellness promotes physical and mental health, leadership, and social well-being to children and adults across Denver and surrounding cities.

Our Impact

5800 children & their families learned to swim. Every year ViVe teaches over 500 kids & their families how to swim through our After School & Learn to Swim programs.

4900 children enrolled in After School and outdoor recreation programs. Movement, social emotional and wellness & Youth Workforce development.

VIVE has supported 15,000 through resources such as Food bags, Telefones, Bus passes, Mental health resources, Children's school and has housed over 4500 people a total of 1000 leases.
