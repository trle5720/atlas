Our classes , camps, and events make fun memorable experiences. We strive to enhance the lives of our community by creating programs for everyone.

Summer Camp

Vive Wellness has different sites with activities this summer. Kids that enroll in our summer camp program will be part of Learn to Swim, games, art, and mindfulness to fill their summer break with movement and fun activities for all.

Outdoor Recreation

Our outdoor recreation program connects Latinx youth and families with nature through hiking, camping, and rafting, many for the first time. In partnership with the City of Denver Outdoor Recreation, Colorado State Parks, and GOCO, we celebrate cultural connections to the outdoors while building skills and developing the next generation of Latino outdoor leaders.

Learn to Swim

Our Swim classes are offered in Spanish and English by certified swim instructors, creating a comfortable environment for youth to learn. Swimming can be a lifesaving skill, yet nearly 60% of Latino children can't swim. Through partnerships with the City of Denver, classes are held at Manual High School, Montbello, and Aerobics pools. Graduates often invite family and friends to enroll, growing our community of swimmers.

After School

Our after-school program supports the physical, emotional and social health of students through swim training, martial arts, science projects, and more, with future plans to expand to additional community schools. Our summer wellness camp offers half-day sessions packed with movement activities, tennis, swimming, and outdoor games, providing fun enrichment for school-aged kids all summer long.

Community Physical Activity

Community fitness programs are led and taught by certified fitness instructors and are offered in both English and Spanish. We offer classes in Zumba, hiking, dance, Zumba, yoga, spinning, running are provided to all ages which often lead to participating together creating connection beyond age.

Eating Healthy

With support from the City of Denver's Healthy Food Denver Kids program, we provide healthy food to more than 2,000 youth and families each month. Through our Montbello community garden and Conexio Saludable program, families across the city help plant, grow, and harvest vegetables — with the produce shared directly with participating families and those facing food insecurity.

Community Events

Our community events bring families and local organizations together at neighborhood parks to access wellness resources, including blood pressure and A1C testing, COVID-19 vaccinations, and mental health education covering topics like stress, depression, and connecting with local professionals. Our community events complement outreach efforts with half-day sessions offering movement activities, tennis, swimming, and outdoor games for school-aged kids.

Mental Health Wellness

Our Mental Health Wellness program centers the needs of teens while honoring cultural beliefs and traditions. Through Teen Mental Health First Aid, students in grades 10-12 learn to recognize and respond to mental health and substance use challenges among their peers. Our Art, Mindfulness, and Movement (AMM) program complements this through breathing techniques, yoga, drawing, journaling, and painting — helping community members tell their stories while reducing stress, easing anxiety, and improving overall physical and mental well-being.

Workforce Development

ViVe's programs are designed as pathways to careers. Youth who complete Learn to Swim can pursue CPR and first aid certification to become lifeguards or swim instructors — many of whom are hired through our City of Denver partnership. Garden program participants can find opportunities with Denver Parks, while fitness participants can pursue careers as instructors or personal trainers. Across all programs, participants have the opportunity to turn their experience into employment, bringing financial stability to themselves and their families.

Older Adult Program

The Older Adults in Action program supports people over 50 years of age in improving their physical and emotional health through education, activities and offering community resources to have a healthier and more successful life.

Serve Kids Better Denver

ViVe Wellness leads Serve Kids Better Denver — a coalition of 40+ organizations, Denver Health, and the American Heart Association tackling health inequities and chronic disease. We're championing a policy to make water, milk, or sparkling water the default drinks on kids' menus, since a single 12 oz soda exceeds the daily recommended added sugar for kids. Our youth and families are driving this change through outreach and advocacy. Want to get involved? Let us know!
